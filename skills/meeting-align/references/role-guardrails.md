# Role-specific guardrails

A role guardrail states what a role must not turn the shared direction into.

Use at most one or two unless the meeting explicitly sets more. Derive each guardrail only from:

1. an explicit scope exclusion;
2. a rejected option;
3. a repeated strategic boundary;
4. a high-confidence execution implication necessary to preserve the agreed direction.

Record the statement, basis, evidence pointer, and whether it is explicit or inferred. Label inferred guardrails `ROLE_GUARDRAIL`.

Valid example:

> Direction: stay asset-light and use partners for phase-one delivery. Operations guardrail: do not build a permanent delivery team in phase one.

Invalid example:

> Do not use junior employees.

The invalid restriction does not follow from the shared direction. Do not invent prohibitions as generic management advice.
