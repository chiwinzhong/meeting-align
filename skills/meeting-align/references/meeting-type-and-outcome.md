# Meeting type and outcome state

Read this reference before extracting actions.

## Primary types

| Type | Primary purpose | Healthy output standard |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `STRATEGY_CO_CREATION` | Explore or converge on direction, positioning, business model, or boundaries | Direction, boundaries, hypotheses, open questions, next validation |
| `DECISION_MEETING` | Choose among known alternatives | Decision, rationale, rejected alternatives, consequences |
| `PROJECT_KICKOFF` | Convert approved direction into coordinated execution | Owners, milestones, definitions of done, dependencies, first actions |
| `EXECUTION_STATUS` | Review progress and resolve blockers | Status, deviations, blockers, decisions, next actions |
| `REVIEW_RETROSPECTIVE` | Evaluate outcomes and extract learning | Evidence, causes, lessons, changes, follow-up |
| `SALES_CLIENT` | Discover needs, align expectations, or make external commitments | Needs, promise boundary, open conditions, authorized commitments |
| `INTERVIEW_DISCOVERY` | Extract knowledge, requirements, experience, or evidence | Source claims, uncertainty, patterns, follow-up questions |
| `BRAINSTORM_IDEATION` | Expand possibilities without requiring convergence | Ideas, clusters, constraints, selection or validation path |

## Mixed meetings

Select one primary type and optional secondary types. When the meeting changes mode, record the transition and apply the appropriate standard to each segment. Do not let a short kickoff segment make an exploratory meeting look execution-ready.

## Confidence

Use `high`, `medium`, or `low` and cite the cue briefly. Keep the type provisional when purpose or mode is ambiguous.

## Outcome states

- `EXPLORING` — important possibilities or evidence are still being generated.
- `DIRECTION_SET` — a direction or boundary is shared, but execution choices remain open.
- `EXECUTION_READY` — scope, authority, owners, and necessary acceptance conditions support action.
- `IN_EXECUTION` — work is active and the meeting controls progress, blockers, or changes.
- `REVIEWING` — the meeting evaluates completed work or outcomes.

Meeting type and outcome state are different. A strategy meeting can be successful at `DIRECTION_SET`; a kickoff normally should reach `EXECUTION_READY`.
