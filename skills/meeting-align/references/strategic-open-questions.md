# Strategic open questions

Read this reference for strategy, co-creation, discovery, or mixed meetings that have direction but are not execution-ready.

Rank questions by blocking power, not by conversational frequency. Prioritize unresolved choices that can change:

- target user or customer;
- product, offer, or value proposition;
- positioning, naming, or promise;
- economics, ownership, or entity structure;
- partner standard or delivery model;
- scope, safety, compliance, or authority;
- first validation step or next commitment point.

For each strategic open question record:

1. the question;
2. the direction already agreed;
3. why the unresolved choice blocks execution;
4. the next evidence, decision, or validation needed;
5. an owner only when assigned or clearly authorized;
6. an evidence pointer.

Say `Direction is aligned; execution is not yet fully specified` when that is the supported state. Do not answer the open question with AI judgment.
