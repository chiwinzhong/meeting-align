# Alignment Score

The Alignment Score is an optional explanatory diagnostic. It summarizes visible clarity across decisions, ownership, deadlines, definitions of done, dependencies, and cross-role interpretation.

Every deduction must link to a specific gap. A score without inspectable deductions should not be shown.

The score is not validated as a scientific measure of people, culture, intelligence, meeting quality, or organizational performance. It must never be used for employee ranking, compensation, discipline, or surveillance.

Field teams should calibrate weights against their own meeting types, publish the rubric used, and report score changes separately from business outcomes.
