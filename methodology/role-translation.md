# Role translation

Role translation answers: “What does the same confirmed decision require from this function?” It must never answer a different question: “What decision would make more sense for this function?”

Every role brief uses six fields:

1. what matters to the role;
2. what the shared decision means for the role;
3. actions supported by the transcript;
4. the priority that must not drift;
5. the stated definition of done, or `not yet defined`;
6. dependencies and handoffs.

A good brief is short enough to review in under three minutes, retains evidence pointers for high-impact claims, and ends with one explicit understanding check.

Professional implications may be inferred when clearly labeled. New strategy, commitments, deadlines, owners, or acceptance criteria may not be inferred.
