# MeetingAlign methodology

MeetingAlign is based on one practical observation: a summary can be accurate while the team is still not aligned.

Its method has five stages:

1. **Reconstruct the meeting.** Read the complete record and preserve sequence, speakers, and source pointers.
2. **Classify evidence.** Separate confirmed decisions and facts from rejected options, deferred items, open questions, inferences, and AI suggestions.
3. **Build one Meeting Truth.** Record scope, actions, owners, deadlines, definitions of done, dependencies, and missing fields in one canonical source.
4. **Translate by role.** Derive role implications without changing the underlying decision.
5. **Expose and confirm gaps.** Surface only ambiguities that can materially change execution, then ask each role for an explicit correction or confirmation.

The core unit is not a paragraph of notes. It is a traceable claim with a type, source, execution consequence, and review status.
